4 Recipients

The chosen school always receives the data it needs to reply. TheGlidery’s team has access for support and follow-up. Technical providers process data on TheGlidery’s behalf: hosting and network (Cloudflare), database (Cloudflare D1) and sending of emails (Resend). Official partners and suppliers receive data only if you ticked box 2. TheGlidery does not sell your data.

5 Transfers outside the European Union and Switzerland

Some providers may process data outside the European Union, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, in particular in the United States. TheGlidery makes sure suitable safeguards are in place: the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

6 Retention periods

Requests are kept for three years after the last exchange with you [to be confirmed]. Proof of authorisations is kept for five years after the end of the processing concerned [to be confirmed]. Technical logs are kept for twelve months at most. After these periods, data is deleted or made anonymous.

7 Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction, its erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and you can object to processing, in particular to marketing. You can withdraw the authorisations of boxes 1 and 2 at any time, without effect on past processing. In France you can also give instructions about what happens to your data after your death. Write to contact@locawing.fr; you will get an answer within one month. You can complain to the supervisory authority of your country: CNIL (France), Data Protection Authority (Belgium), Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (Switzerland).

8 Schools

Each school is responsible for the data it receives to handle your request and informs you of its own processing. When you buy from a school, read its privacy information.

9 Minors

Requests are made by an adult. For a minor passenger, the legal representative provides the data and exercises the rights.
