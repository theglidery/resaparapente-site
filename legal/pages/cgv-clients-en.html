2 Role of the site

The site is a free directory. It shows tandem flight and course offers published by schools, lets visitors compare them, and passes booking requests and gift requests to the chosen school free of charge.

TheGlidery is neither the organiser nor the seller of the services. The site sells nothing and asks for no payment. The booking and, where relevant, the gift voucher are agreed directly between the customer and the school.

3 Offers, prices and published information

The listings reproduce the information that schools provide or publish: services, places, durations, conditions and prices. A “from” price is an indicative starting price. When no price is published, the site shows “Price on request”. Prices are the school’s own, in euros or Swiss francs depending on its country. Dates and places may change until the school confirms them.

This information is collected from schools and public sources. It may contain errors or be out of date. Only the school’s confirmation counts. The customer can report incorrect information to contact@locawing.fr; TheGlidery corrects or removes it promptly.

4 Booking request

To request a tandem flight or a course, the customer fills in the form of the chosen offer: first name, last name, email, phone, passenger weight (the school needs it to choose the wing), preferred date and, in case of postponement, two other dates. For a course, the customer gives the preferred start and the weight and height of each participant. Before sending, the customer ticks the box accepting the terms (Annex B).

The acknowledgement confirms that the request has been passed to the school. The request is free and non-binding, for the customer and for the school. It is not a booking. The school replies to the customer with the service, the time slot, the price including tax, the payment terms and the applicable conditions, including cancellation. The booking is agreed directly between the customer and the school once they agree.

The request is sent to the school’s email address. When a school has no usable address, the request is sent to the ResaParapente team (contact@locawing.fr), which passes it to the school by hand. The reply may then take longer. Without a reply, the customer can contact the school or TheGlidery directly.

5 Giving a tandem flight or a course

The “Give as a Gift” button lets the customer give a published formula to someone else. The giver first enters their own contact details (first name, last name, email, phone), then the passenger’s (first name, last name, email, phone), the passenger’s weight, an approximate preferred flight date and, if they wish, a message for the passenger.

By sending the request, the giver confirms that they have told the passenger that their contact details are passed to the chosen school to organise the gift.

As for a booking, TheGlidery passes the request to the school, without payment or commitment. The school contacts the giver, gives the price including tax and the payment terms, issues and delivers the gift voucher or confirms the service, and states its validity and its conditions of use, postponement and cancellation. The voucher is sold and issued by the school, which is responsible for it. Some schools offer their own gift voucher: the offer is marked “Voucher offered by the school” and remains sold by the school.

TheGlidery uses the passenger’s contact details only to pass the request to the school. They are not used for any marketing.

6 Payment, postponement, cancellation and withdrawal

Payment is made to the school, on its terms. TheGlidery receives no payment and can neither collect, refund nor exchange a voucher.

The school handles changes, postponements, cancellations, refunds and, where relevant, withdrawal, according to the contract made with the customer, its own terms of sale and the applicable law (Annex A). Before paying, the customer reads these conditions. These terms do not reduce any right that the law gives to consumers.

In several countries, the fourteen-day right of withdrawal for distance contracts does not apply to leisure activities to be provided on a specific date or during a specific period. The applicable law and the school state what applies to each purchase.

7 Safety and conditions of participation

Before booking, the school states the age, weight, level and equipment conditions and any other relevant requirements. The passenger gives the school accurate information, including weight, height and anything relevant to the safety of the flight, and follows the instructions of the staff. For a minor, the school states the authorisations and supervision required.

The school and the pilot make the operational and safety decisions, including postponing, adapting or interrupting the flight or course. If participation is refused, the school states the reason and handles the booking according to the conditions given and the applicable law. These terms preserve the customer’s rights where a service is not provided, is interrupted or is poorly performed.

Paragliding is an aerial activity with risks. Before booking, the school informs the passenger of these risks, of the insurance that covers the flight and of possible additional insurance.
