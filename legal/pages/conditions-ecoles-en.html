Part II Listing agreement with schools

Draft · Version [date]

1 Parties and purpose

Between TheGlidery SAS, publisher of resaparapente.fr, resaparapente.ch and resaparapente.be, a simplified joint-stock company with a share capital of €30,000, registered office in Saint-Martin-d’Uriage (38410, France) [full address], Grenoble register number 943 359 984, represented by [name and title], and [school’s company name], [legal form], [address], [registration number: SIREN in France, UID in Switzerland, enterprise number (BCE/KBO) in Belgium], represented by [name and title], hereinafter “the School”.

The partnership is non-exclusive. It covers the presentation of the School’s offers in the directory, the passing on of the booking requests and “Give as a Gift” requests that customers send, and the display of the promotional prices that the School provides. The School sets the conditions of its offers, replies to requests, sells its services and vouchers, and agrees bookings with its customers.

Listing and passing on requests are free for the School. Any paid option (such as a featured placement) would need a separate written agreement and would be marked as such to the public.

2 Listings, prices and updates

The School validates the information displayed for its offers and tells TheGlidery as soon as possible of any change in price, formula, requirements, contact details, availability or calendar. The prices provided include tax and all mandatory fees. Optional extras and their price are shown separately. TheGlidery promptly corrects or removes information reported as incorrect. When the School provides no price, the listing shows “Price on request”.

3 Booking requests and “Give as a Gift” requests

The School receives requests at [receiving email address]. Each request contains the customer’s contact details, the passenger’s weight, the preferred dates (one preferred date and two alternative dates for a tandem flight) and, for a course, the weight and height of each participant. A “Give as a Gift” request also contains the contact details of the giver and of the passenger, and an approximate flight date. If the School has no usable receiving address, requests go to the ResaParapente team, which passes them to the School by hand.

The School replies to the customer as soon as possible, in principle within two working days [to be confirmed]. Before the customer commits, it gives the service, the price including tax, the place, the date, the conditions of participation, payment and cancellation, and the applicable complaint and mediation information. For a “Give as a Gift” request, it issues and delivers the gift voucher or confirms the service, and states its validity and its conditions of use, postponement and cancellation.

4 Roles of the parties, payment and liability

Each booking and each voucher is agreed directly between the School and its customer. The School collects the price and handles payments, refunds, postponements, cancellations and withdrawal according to the applicable law. TheGlidery receives no payment, does not act as the School’s agent and is not a party to these contracts.

The School is responsible for the quality and delivery of its services. TheGlidery is responsible for the operation of its site and its own communications. Each party is liable for damage that it causes through its fault to the other party or to a customer.

5 Qualifications, safety and insurance

The School certifies that it holds the qualifications, professional cards, declarations and authorisations that the rules of the country where it operates require for the activities offered, and that it checks those of its staff. It maintains liability insurance covering the activities actually offered. It gives TheGlidery a valid certificate before its listing is activated and at each renewal.

The School tells TheGlidery immediately of a suspension, a change of cover or an incident that makes an offer unavailable. It manages the weather, participants’ fitness, equipment, instructions and decisions to fly or to stop. It informs the customer of the risks of the activity and of the insurance that covers the flight.

6 Content, ranking and promotional prices

The School authorises TheGlidery to use its name, its logo and the content it provides or validates to present its offers on the site during the partnership. It confirms that it holds the necessary rights. TheGlidery may format this content and corrects or removes incorrect or disputed information.

Ranking follows the filters chosen by the visitor, then ascending price; offers with no published price come last. At the date of the agreement, no payment influences the ranking [to be confirmed]. Any paid placement is marked as such to the public. The School may provide promotional prices including tax, the formulas and slots concerned, the access conditions and the validity period; TheGlidery shows them on its card during that period, free of charge.

7 Personal data

The School receives customers’ data to reply to their request and to agree and deliver the service. It uses them for that purpose. Any other use, including marketing, is its own responsibility and requires its own legal basis. It does not pass this data to third parties for commercial purposes without a legal basis.

Each party is responsible for the processing for which it decides the purposes and means. It informs the people concerned, protects the data, applies suitable retention periods and answers requests to exercise rights that concern it. The School informs TheGlidery without delay of a data breach that affects requests passed on by the site. If one party processes data only on the other’s instructions, the parties sign the required processing agreement. The applicable law is that of the School’s country (GDPR in France and Belgium, the Federal Act on Data Protection in Switzerland).

8 Availability of the service and force majeure

TheGlidery works to keep the site available without guaranteeing continuous availability. It may interrupt it for maintenance. Neither party is liable for a failure caused by force majeure.

9 Term, suspension and end of the agreement

The agreement is concluded for an indefinite term. Either party may end it in writing with thirty days’ notice, subject to mandatory periods. For a breach that can be remedied, the party concerned has fifteen days from formal notice to put it right. TheGlidery may suspend an offer in case of incorrect information, an inactive link, an expired insurance certificate or a safety risk, in accordance with the applicable rules.

Requests for correction, removal or dispute go to contact@locawing.fr. At the end of the agreement, TheGlidery removes the offers and content authorised solely for the partnership. The School continues to handle the bookings and vouchers already agreed.

10 Governing law and disputes

The agreement is governed by French law. The parties try to settle their dispute in writing, then go to the courts of Grenoble, subject to mandatory rules to the contrary. This procedure leaves each party free to ask for the urgent measures that the law allows.
